Our Desert Experiences

Discover the real Wadi Rum through personal experiences, breathtaking landscapes and traditional Bedouin hospitality. Every visit is an opportunity to experience the desert in a special way.

4x4 Jeep Tours

Explore red sand dunes, sandstone mountains, ancient inscriptions, natural rock bridges and spectacular desert landscapes by 4x4 jeep.

Hidden Valleys

Discover quieter corners of Wadi Rum away from the busy routes. Enjoy gentle walks through beautiful valleys and canyons surrounded by magnificent mountains.

Sunset Experience

Watch the desert change colour as the sun disappears behind the mountains. Relax, enjoy the peaceful atmosphere and take in the beauty of the desert.

Camel Rides

Meet Your Local Bedouin Guide

Marhaba! My name is Nawaf. I was born and raised in Wadi Rum, Jordan, and I have around ten years of experience welcoming visitors to my homeland.

I love sharing the beauty of the desert, its hidden places, Bedouin traditions and the stories of our land. For me, a desert tour is more than visiting famous places. It is an opportunity to experience the atmosphere and lifestyle of Wadi Rum.

My goal is to offer personal experiences, peaceful moments away from busy routes and genuine Bedouin hospitality. I look forward to welcoming you to my desert home.
